## Settled-print rounding

A read-only re-query of Hyperliquid `fundingHistory` checked all 36 settled-print rows across the nine daily reports from 2026-10-04 through 2026-10-12. Counts and negative-hour totals matched. Fourteen rows showed the exact protocol-floor rate as `10.9%/yr`; the hourly value `0.0000125` annualizes to exactly `10.95%/yr`, which rounds half-up to `11.0%/yr` at one decimal. The old generator's binary floating-point `toFixed(1)` displayed those exact-floor values as `10.9`; the verifier and generator now use exact decimal rounding, and the affected values are corrected in the current tree.

## Withdrawn 180-day figures and screen labels

The `180d avg` values in the historical digest text came from hard-coded constants in `funding-digest.mjs`; the underlying series was not archived in this repository. They are withdrawn and must not be used as current evidence.

The historical `DEPLOY`, `HOLD`, and `watch` labels were generated from rate thresholds and a static screen list. They did not prove that a position was open or an order was executed. The report generator's raw watchdog input is private and is not present in this repository. Treat those samples as operator telemetry, not independently reproduced results or realized P&L.

## Account snapshots are not AUM

The files named `reports/reserves-*.md` were mislabeled as "Proof of Reserves" and "TOTAL AUM." They aggregate listed operating, bridge, buyer/test, and Hyperliquid account balances. They are not an independent audit and do not establish client AUM. At the time of this errata, the checked-in `aum-ledger.json` recorded no depositors and `$0` deposited with intake paused; that dated ledger is not a current deposit-status feed.

For 2026-10-12, the displayed component rows sum to `$13.4977`, or `$13.50` to two decimals. The original `$13.49` total cannot be reconciled because the unrounded source inputs were not archived. The current report marks that total as unreconciled rather than presenting it as AUM.
